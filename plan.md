# Research Plan: Practical AI Catalogue

## Overall Context
Catalogue how current multimodal AI (text, image, audio, video) can be practically applied to business problems. The goal is a living reference that maps business functions to AI capabilities, tools, and deployment patterns — with honest assessments of maturity, cost, and limitations.

## Current Work To-Dos
- [ ] Define business function taxonomy (sales, marketing, ops, support, finance, HR, product, legal)
- [ ] Map AI modalities to business functions (text generation, vision, speech, code, agents)
- [ ] Inventory current tools/providers per capability
- [ ] Evaluate maturity levels and real-world feasibility

## Current Hypotheses
1. **H1 — Multimodal AI is production-ready for more tasks than businesses assume**: Most businesses are only using text LLMs; vision, audio, and agentic workflows are underutilized.
2. **H2 — Integration complexity, not model capability, is the bottleneck**: The main barrier is plumbing (APIs, pipelines, UIs), not what models can do.
3. **H3 — Commodity tooling exists for 80% of common use cases**: Open-source + API solutions cover most needs; custom training is rarely required.
